About Us · Why Choose Us

Three and a half decades of brick, and still hands-on.

We are Tejmal Kashyap and Kartik Kashyap. For about 36 years, our family has made bricks here in Bhangia, Indore. What began as a small kiln has grown into a name that masons and builders across the city ask for.

Our father's rule was simple: a brick should never embarrass the wall it sits in. That is why every V.K brick carries a raised logo. If we put our name on it, we stand behind it.

Today we work side by side, one generation teaching the next. Call us and one of us will pick up.

  • About 36 years of brick-making experience
  • Raised V.K logo embossed on every brick
  • Strong red clay bricks, 4×8 and slim sizes
  • Bulk supply and orders planned to your date
  • Two owners reachable directly by phone
